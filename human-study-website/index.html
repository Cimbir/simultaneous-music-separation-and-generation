Music Listening Study

You will listen to short AI-generated music clips and rank them. It takes about 10 minutes.

  • The clips are ~8 seconds of multi-instrument music (bass, drums, guitar, piano).
  • There are no right or wrong answers, just judge by ear.
  • Please finish in one sitting and do not refresh the page.

How it works

Each round shows you 4 clips. Listen to all of them, then rank them on two things:

Musicality

Is this actually music? Does each part (bass, drums, guitar, piano) sound like a real instrument playing a real melody, groove, or rhythm, rather than random or meaningless notes?

Instrument coherence

How well do the instruments fit together, as if playing the same song?

  • Play each clip, and replay it up to 3 times.
  • Drag the cards (or use the ▲ ▼ buttons) to order them from best (top) to worst (bottom).
  • You must play every clip before you can continue.
