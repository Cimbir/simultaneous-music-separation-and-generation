Music Listening Study

You will listen to short AI-generated music clips and rank them by two separate criteria. It takes about 10 minutes.

  • The clips are ~8 seconds of multi-instrument music (bass, drums, guitar, piano).
  • There are no right or wrong answers, just judge by ear.
  • Please finish in one sitting and do not refresh the page.

How it works

Each round shows you 5 clips. Listen to all of them, then rank them twice:

Realism

How realistic does the track sound? Does it sound like it was recorded by real people or generated by a computer?

Instrument coherence

How well do the instruments work together as a group? Does each one fit naturally into the overall arrangement, or do they clash and sound like they don't belong in the same track?

  • Play each clip, and replay it up to 5 times.
  • Drag the cards (or use the ▲ ▼ buttons) to order them from best (top) to worst (bottom).
  • A clip can be good on one criterion and bad on the other. Rank each criterion independently.

Listen to the difference

These three examples show that realism and coherence are different. Play all three before the practice round.

  • For Realism, focus on how realistic the instruments sound in the track.
  • For Instrument coherence, focus on whether the instruments belong together and form a cohesive track.
  • Do not average the two answers. The same clip may deserve different rankings for realism and coherence.

Play all three examples before practice.
